### 🧑‍🔬 About Me

My research sits at the intersection of **AI for Science**, **agentic systems**, and **biomedicine**, with publications in **Cell**, **Science**, and **Nature**.

I am advised by [Qiang Gao](https://hupi.fudan.edu.cn/en/rcdw/rc_content.jsp?urltype=news.NewsContentUrl&wbtreeid=1097&wbnewsid=1422) (Fudan), [Le Cong](https://profiles.stanford.edu/186687) (Stanford), and [Mengdi Wang](https://mwang-delta.vercel.app/) (Princeton).

**I believe in AI.** I build agents & world models for the transition to programmable medicine.
